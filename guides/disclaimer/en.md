## Read this before you open your city to people

FiveM runs on top of **Grand Theft Auto V** by Rockstar Games. FiveM is made by Cfx.re, which operates under the **Rockstar Games Creator Program License Agreement**. That agreement lets you run your own server, but it puts obligations on you as the operator — not on us.

> [!danger]
> Your server, its name, its content and its community are your responsibility. We rent you the server and nothing more. We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or Cfx.re.

## What you must publish

The agreement requires two things wherever you present your server — your site, your Discord, your server page:

1. **An email contact.** The agreement asks for a valid email address for you, or for whoever operates the server, written somewhere clear and obvious. A Discord invite is a good extra, but it does not replace the email.
2. **A clear notice that Rockstar has nothing to do with your server.** Copy this text as it is:

```txt
This server is not approved, sponsored, or endorsed by Rockstar Games,
Take-Two Interactive, or Cfx.re.
```

And in Arabic beside it:

```txt
هذا السيرفر غير معتمد ولا مدعوم ولا معتمَد من Rockstar Games
أو Take-Two Interactive أو Cfx.re.
```

Put it in your Discord description and in your in-game server description.

## What is not allowed

These are the most common reasons servers get shut down. Stay on the right side from day one:

- **Do not sell game content.** You may take donations or subscriptions for perks in your own community, but you may not sell Rockstar's content.
- **Do not use Rockstar trademarks** in your server name or logo in a way that suggests they endorse you.
- **Do not use ripped content** from other games or other servers.
- **Every player needs a genuine copy of GTA V.** FiveM checks this — do not try to work around it.

## The licence key is yours

The key you paste into the Licence tab comes from your own account on [portal.cfx.re](https://portal.cfx.re). That means the server is registered to you at Cfx.re, and any violation lands on you.

This is deliberate: your city is yours, and you can move it anywhere else whenever you like.

## What happens if you break the rules

Cfx.re can suspend your key, and your server stops running even while your Serverk plan is still active. Rockstar also reserves the right to designate which hosting providers are authorised.

If a rights holder sends us a formal complaint about your server, we will tell you and give you time to deal with it.
